⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida — is the only missable trophy in the game.

The first meditation is the trap: Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house, in Chapter 1 only. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time), follow her out to the balcony and accept the optional task. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends, there is no mission replay, and the trophy needs all 18 spots — so do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 manual save until the trophy pops.

The road to the Platinum — 4 stages

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story. Finish all five chapters and the final mission. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, the time-sensitive Race Against Yago in Chapter 3, and keep a manual save from just before the final mission.
  2. Stage 2 — Open world & activities. Bank vaults, races, stunts, shooting ranges, shops, garages and the stranger strands. Most of the grind trophies live here.
  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & 100%. Card sets, WAiNK scans, vehicles and the map. The 100% checklist (A Long Road) only appears after the story, and it is lenient — roughly 60% of everything.
  4. Stage 4 — Cleanup & Platinum. Mop up whatever the in-game checklist still shows, then Vice Got a Price! pops on its own as the capstone.
