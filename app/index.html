Roadmap to the Platinum

Built from the official trophy descriptions: the order to earn them in, which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts. One playthrough is enough — there are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. Guides put the Platinum at roughly 200 hours.

    The one trophy you can miss: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots). One meditation, on Lucia’s balcony, is only available in Chapter 1, and the game has no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Source: KeenGamer, GTAVice.

      About this guide

      Every trophy’s name, official description and grade come from the official PlayStation 5 list (55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned), as listed by GTAVice and KeenGamer, which agree on every name, description and grade. Each guide below is written in our own words and names its sources beside it; nothing is copied from another guide. Five hidden trophies still have no confirmed unlock method at launch — their cards say so plainly instead of guessing.

      This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games. It uses no Rockstar logo, official artwork or game screenshots.
