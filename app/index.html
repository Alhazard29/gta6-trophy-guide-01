Read this first: the one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. There are 18 meditation spots, and the Chapter 1 spot at Jason’s house / Lucia’s balcony disappears after Chapter 1, so meditate there before finishing the chapter, and keep manual saves. This is the only trophy reported as possibly missable.

Five methods are unconfirmed — no guide has published verified steps for them yet: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their cards are marked METHOD UNCONFIRMED and do not guess.
