Read this first: the one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable trophy on the list. It asks you to meditate at every spot across Leonida — and the meditation spot at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 (Lucia’s balcony) disappears once Chapter 1 ends. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save from the start of every chapter, and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Every other trophy can be earned after the credits.

Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method yet — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their cards say so plainly and give only the official description as a hint.

Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours in all; the long pole is A Long Road (100% completion). Work the list in this order and nothing is left to chance.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–4 and the finale

    Twelve story trophies pop as you play: Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Chapter 1), T**s Ass Drug Money (Chapter 2), Vice City, Baby! (Chapter 3), Top Of The World, Ma (Chapter 4) and You Gotta Know When to Get Off (the final mission), plus the mission trophies Trust (Breakfast Run), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist), Family Matters (Child’s Play), DWNFALL (Headliners), Hi-Jacked (VCIA Takedown) and Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory). While you are here: do Lucia’s balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends (Positive Mental Attitude) — it is the only thing on the whole list that can be missed.

  2. Stage 2 — Strangers & side stories

    Finish the stranger questlines: Andy (Road to Rehabilitation), Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand), Tyrone (Spin On This!) and Bret’s hunting (Fair Game). Eight stranger strands complete Strangers & Freaks. All of these stay available after the credits, so work through them in any order you like.

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles and activities

    Meditation spots (Positive Mental Attitude — 18 spots, remember the Chapter 1 one), 3 card sets (Don’t Touch My Deck), 100 WAiNK locations (You WAiNKer!), 100 vehicles (Whip It Good), 50 challenges (One Last Thing), 250 stunts (Cunning Stunts), gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), a drone-race win at all 14 locations (Top Fun), 20 different race wins (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), beating Cal at fishing (Baited My Tackle), one arcade game (Stick Fiddler), exploring all 52 areas of Leonida (The Goodtime State), and the rest of the activity list: Master of None (try all 9 activity types once), Gunslinger, Catch Me If You Can, Challenging Tank, Let’s Go Bowling, Ten Pin, Little Boxes (all 8 garages) and On the Spot (10 Scooter Bros registrations).

  4. Stage 4 — Robbery, money and the platinum

    Five bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal), one armored truck (A Classic Blitz Play), three shops back-to-back with your partner (Retail Therapy), one jewelry store undetected (Sneakthief), an Illegal Operation for each of the nine gangs (Pest Control), $250,000 of Marked Bills laundered (Dime on the Dollar), $100,000 spent (Treat Yo Self), and the loose ends: Hot Together, Personal Best and Actions Speak Louder. Then check Progress > Stats > 100% Completion in game and mop up whatever is left — that is A Long Road, the hardest trophy on the list. When it pops, Vice Got a Price! follows: congratulations, you’re a Leonida Legend. (Three hidden trophies — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There — have no confirmed method yet; their cards say so.)
