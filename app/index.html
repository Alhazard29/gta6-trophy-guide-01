⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots). Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter ends. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time): she heads out to the balcony of Jason’s house to meditate — follow her and accept the optional task. There is no mission replay, so keep a Chapter 1 save until this trophy pops.

Platinum roadmap

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story: finish the Prologue and Chapters 1–5, doing Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 before it ends, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter plus one before the final mission. There is no mission replay.
  2. Stage 2 — Side stories: finish the final missions for Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy and Tyrone, Bret’s hunting questline, and 8 Strangers & Freaks strands.
  3. Stage 3 — Open-world cleanup: stunts, races, shooting ranges, drone races, collectibles, robberies and exploration, tracking progress under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.
  4. Stage 4 — 100% completion: attain 100% Completion for A Long Road, then the Platinum pops.

Safety rules: never use cheats on the trophy save (cheats disable trophies and saving), and keep manual saves because there is no New Game Plus.
