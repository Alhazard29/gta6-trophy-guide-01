Read this first: the one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable trophy on the list. It asks you to meditate at every spot across Leonida — and Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears once Chapter 1 ends. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save from the start of every chapter, and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Every other trophy can be earned after the credits.

Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their cards say so plainly and give only the official description as a hint.

Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours in all; the long pole is A Long Road (100% completion). Work the list in this order and nothing is left to chance.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–4 and the finale

    Twelve story trophies pop as you play: Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Chapter 1), T**s Ass Drug Money (Chapter 2), Vice City, Baby! (Chapter 3), Top Of The World, Ma (Chapter 4) and You Gotta Know When to Get Off (the final mission), plus the mission trophies Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked and Snow Storm. While you are here: do Lucia’s balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends (Positive Mental Attitude), do the time-sensitive Race Against Yago the moment it appears in Chapter 3 (hourglass icon — it counts toward Strangers & Freaks), and near the end of Chapter 4 take the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is, which opens the bank vault robberies.

  2. Stage 2 — Strangers & side stories

    Finish the stranger questlines: Andy (Lucia-only, Road to Rehabilitation), Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand), Tyrone (Jason-only, Spin On This!) and Bret’s hunting (Fair Game). Eight stranger strands complete Strangers & Freaks — Andy’s, Billy’s and Tyrone’s own trophies do not count toward that eight, so check the trophy’s progress counter after each strand. All of these stay available after the credits.

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles and activities

    Meditation spots (Positive Mental Attitude), 3 card sets (Don’t Touch My Deck), 100 WAiNK locations (You WAiNKer!), 100 vehicles (Whip It Good — 200 of 277 for 100%), 50 Challenges (One Last Thing — 70 of 80 for 100%), 250 stunts (Cunning Stunts — 300 for 100%), gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), a drone-race win at all 14 locations (Top Fun), 20 different race wins (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), beating Cal at fishing (Baited My Tackle), one arcade game (Stick Fiddler), and maximum Strength with both partners (Personal Best). Try one of every activity type early for Master of None.

  4. Stage 4 — Robbery and money

    Five bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal — all eight for 100%), one armored truck (A Classic Blitz Play), three shops back-to-back with your partner (Retail Therapy), one jewelry store undetected with the Snap Gun from Chapter 3 (Sneakthief), an Illegal Operation for each of the nine gangs (Pest Control — all 39 for 100%), $250,000 of Marked Bills laundered (Dime on the Dollar), $100,000 spent (Treat Yo Self), all eight garages (Little Boxes) and 10 Scooter Bros registrations (On the Spot). Escaping a vault job with your partner also earns Hot Together.

  5. Stage 5 — Clean-up and the platinum

    Check Progress > Stats > 100% Completion in game and mop up whatever is left — that is A Long Road, the hardest trophy on the list. When it pops, Vice Got a Price! follows: congratulations, you’re a Leonida Legend.
